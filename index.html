Sprint 1 Master Architecture Guide

For 12-Developer Distributed Team:

The master dashboard provides a persistent shell. To build any of the 13 remaining business modules in Sprint 2 without modifying this shell, follow the 3-step workflow below.

How to Add a Business Module:
  1. Copy modules/_template/ to modules/<module-id>/
  2. Rename module.js and module.css to <module-id>.js and <module-id>.css.
  3. Edit modules/<module-id>/module.config.js to set status to ready and path to your page. Done: zero changes to core files!

Master shell files (index.html, core/js/shell/*) are owned by the Master Framework Maintainer.
